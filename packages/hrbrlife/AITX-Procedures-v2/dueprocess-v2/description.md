DueProcess v2 is the next-generation compliance casework engine for the cca.sh constellation. It drives Reg E error resolution, CFPB complaint tracking, and an audit hash-chain that makes every case state transition non-repudiable. Cases are routed through configurable procedure templates; outcomes are signed and time-stamped. Multi-role sign-off (maker/checker/reviewer) with per-template quorum policies. Integrates with ccash (operator console), cyberteller (payment slips), and OpenSanctions (screening sidecar) via Grapple capability claims.
